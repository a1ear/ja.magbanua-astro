A community platform where developers and designers publish web templates, app
designs, and UI kits, and the community votes the best to the top with a
yowkbit-based ranking system. Built-in chat includes group calls and screen
sharing.

Currently in alpha, on Next.js and Supabase Postgres, with Cloudflare R2 for
uploads and Upstash for rate limiting.
